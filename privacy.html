Privacy policy

Last updated: 2026-10-10. Controller: the business named in the imprint.

Your pictures

The pattern is made by code that runs in your browser. Your picture is never sent to us or to anyone else.

No accounts, no cookies for tracking

You do not need an account. We do not set tracking cookies.

Counting visits and actions

To learn what works, the site counts anonymous events, for example "page viewed", "pattern made", "checkout started" and "payment completed". Each event adds 1 to a counter at Abacus (abacus.jasoncameron.dev), a free counting service. No personal data is put in these requests, but like any web request, the service can see your IP address. If the owner turns on GoatCounter, it counts page views without cookies in the same way. The site stores small flags in your browser (localStorage): fc_seen (so a returning visitor is counted once), fc_unlock (your unlock and order ID) and noble_exclude (set only by the owner, to skip counting).

Payments

When you open the payment window, the PayPal script loads from paypal.com. PayPal processes your payment as an independent controller under its own privacy statement. We receive your order ID, the amount and the payer details that PayPal shares with sellers.

Hosting

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log IP addresses for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Support messages

If you contact support, we use your message and order ID only to answer you, and keep them for up to 2 years, or longer when tax law requires it for payment records (up to 10 years under German tax law).
