DMC floss color chart: all 489 colors

This is the full list of the 489 DMC six-strand floss colors that FlossChart uses: the DMC number, the color name and an approximate screen color as a hex code. Use it to look up a number from a pattern key, to find a color by name, or to plan a palette before you buy thread.

The table comes from the open color data set craft-color-codes by MakeBead, licensed CC BY 4.0. FlossChart did not change any color value. A screen can only show an approximation of real thread: the same floss looks different in daylight, under a lamp and on another monitor. Before you buy a lot of one color, check the real skein or a printed color card.

How FlossChart picks colors from this table

When you turn a photo into a pattern, FlossChart converts every pixel and every DMC color into CIELAB, a color space built to match how people see differences. It then picks the closest DMC colors and limits the chart to the number of colors you choose (2 to 60). So every symbol on a FlossChart pattern is one of the 489 colors below.

DMC colors that look almost the same

Some DMC colors are very close on screen. These are the 8 closest pairs in the table, measured as the CIELAB difference FlossChart uses (lower means more alike). If a shop is out of one, the other is worth a look, but compare the real thread first.

  • DMC 225 Shell Pink Ultra Very Light and DMC 967 Apricot Very Light: difference 0,7
  • DMC 3865 Winter White and DMC 3866 Mocha Brown Ultra Very Light: difference 0,7
  • DMC 894 Carnation Very Light and DMC 776 Pink Medium: difference 0,9
  • DMC 732 Olive Green and DMC 731 Olive Green Dark: difference 1,0
  • DMC 3781 Mocha Brown Dark and DMC 839 Beige Brown Dark: difference 1,4
  • DMC 3706 Melon Medium and DMC 604 Cranberry Light: difference 1,5
  • DMC 3833 Raspberry Light and DMC 3733 Dusty Rose: difference 1,5
  • DMC 818 Baby Pink and DMC 967 Apricot Very Light: difference 1,6
