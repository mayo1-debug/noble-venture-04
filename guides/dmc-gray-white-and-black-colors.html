Gray, white and black DMC floss colors

FlossChart's DMC table has 54 gray, white and black colors. This page lists all of them with the DMC number, the name and an approximate screen color, from the lightest (DMC B5200, Snow White) to the darkest (DMC 310, Black).

How the list is made: a color is on this page when its DMC name contains one of these words: "gray", "grey", "pewter", "white", "black", "tin", "steel", "ash", "pearl", "snow", "ecru", "beaver". Names are what DMC calls the color, so a few colors show up in two families (for example a "Blue Green" is on the green page and the blue page). Lightness is measured as CIELAB L*, the same color math the FlossChart converter uses.

The biggest gray, white and black color groups

Many DMC colors come in runs of one name from very light to very dark. In this family the biggest runs are: Beaver Gray (6), Beige Gray (5), Brown Gray (5), Gray Green (5), Blue Gray (3). The Beaver Gray run, light to dark, is: Beaver Gray Very Light (3072), Beaver Gray Light (648), Beaver Gray Medium (647), Beaver Gray Dark (646), Beaver Gray Very Dark (645), Beaver Gray Ultra Dark (844).

The closest look-alikes in this family, by the same color difference: DMC 3072 and DMC 762 (difference 1,7); DMC 3024 and DMC 762 (difference 1,7); DMC 415 and DMC 02 (difference 1,7). On screen these are hard to tell apart, so check the real thread before you swap one for another.
