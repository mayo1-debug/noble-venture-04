Brown, beige and tan DMC floss colors

FlossChart's DMC table has 78 brown, beige and tan colors. This page lists all of them with the DMC number, the name and an approximate screen color, from the lightest (DMC 3866, Mocha Brown Ultra Very Light) to the darkest (DMC 3371, Black Brown).

How the list is made: a color is on this page when its DMC name contains one of these words: "brown", "beige", "mocha", "desert", "mahogany", "hazelnut", "drab", "rosewood", "cocoa", "tan", "coffee", "driftwood". Names are what DMC calls the color, so a few colors show up in two families (for example a "Blue Green" is on the green page and the blue page). Lightness is measured as CIELAB L*, the same color math the FlossChart converter uses.

The biggest brown, beige and tan color groups

Many DMC colors come in runs of one name from very light to very dark. In this family the biggest runs are: Desert Sand (7), Beige Brown (6), Mahogany (6), Mocha Brown (6), Beige Gray (5). The Desert Sand run, light to dark, is: Desert Sand Very Light (3774), Desert Sand Light (950), Desert Sand (3064), Desert Sand Medium (407), Desert Sand Dark (3773), Desert Sand Very Dark (3772), Desert Sand Ultra Very Dark (632).

The closest look-alikes in this family, by the same color difference: DMC 3781 and DMC 839 (difference 1,4); DMC 3782 and DMC 842 (difference 1,8); DMC 3864 and DMC 842 (difference 1,9). On screen these are hard to tell apart, so check the real thread before you swap one for another.
