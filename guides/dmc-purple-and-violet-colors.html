Purple, violet and mauve DMC floss colors

FlossChart's DMC table has 60 purple, violet and mauve colors. This page lists all of them with the DMC number, the name and an approximate screen color, from the lightest (DMC 27, White Violet) to the darkest (DMC 35, Fuschia Very Dark).

How the list is made: a color is on this page when its DMC name contains one of these words: "violet", "lavender", "plum", "grape", "mauve", "eggplant", "blueberry", "fuschia", "purple". Names are what DMC calls the color, so a few colors show up in two families (for example a "Blue Green" is on the green page and the blue page). Lightness is measured as CIELAB L*, the same color math the FlossChart converter uses.

The biggest purple, violet and mauve color groups

Many DMC colors come in runs of one name from very light to very dark. In this family the biggest runs are: Blue Violet (7), Lavender (7), Antique Mauve (6), Plum (6), Violet (6). The Blue Violet run, light to dark, is: Blue Violet Very Light (3747), Blue Violet Light (341), Blue Violet Medium Light (156), Blue Violet Medium (340), Blue Violet Medium Dark (155), Blue Violet Dark (3746), Blue Violet Very Dark (333).

The closest look-alikes in this family, by the same color difference: DMC 24 and DMC 25 (difference 1.9); DMC 341 and DMC 3840 (difference 3.6); DMC 3837 and DMC 327 (difference 3.6). On screen these are hard to tell apart, so check the real thread before you swap one for another.
