Blue and turquoise DMC floss colors

FlossChart's DMC table has 89 blue and turquoise colors. This page lists all of them with the DMC number, the name and an approximate screen color, from the lightest (DMC 3756, Baby Blue Ultra Very Light) to the darkest (DMC 939, Navy Blue Very Dark).

How the list is made: a color is on this page when its DMC name contains one of these words: "blue", "turquoise", "cornflower", "wedgewood", "peacock", "delft", "aquamarine", "navy", "sky", "teal", "electric", "royal". Names are what DMC calls the color, so a few colors show up in two families (for example a "Blue Green" is on the green page and the blue page). Lightness is measured as CIELAB L*, the same color math the FlossChart converter uses.

The biggest blue and turquoise color groups

Many DMC colors come in runs of one name from very light to very dark. In this family the biggest runs are: Baby Blue (9), Turquoise (9), Blue Violet (7), Cornflower Blue (7), Antique Blue (6). The Baby Blue run, light to dark, is: Baby Blue Ultra Very Light (3756), Baby Blue Very Light (775), Baby Blue Pale (3841), Baby Blue Light (3325), Baby Blue (3755), Baby Blue Medium (334), Baby Blue Dark (322), Baby Blue Very Dark (312), Baby Blue Ultra Very Dark (803).

The closest look-alikes in this family, by the same color difference: DMC 162 and DMC 775 (difference 1,7); DMC 826 and DMC 334 (difference 2,2); DMC 793 and DMC 161 (difference 2,3). On screen these are hard to tell apart, so check the real thread before you swap one for another.
