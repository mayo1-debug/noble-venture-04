Yellow and gold DMC floss colors

FlossChart's DMC table has 58 yellow and gold colors. This page lists all of them with the DMC number, the name and an approximate screen color, from the lightest (DMC 3823, Yellow Ultra Pale) to the darkest (DMC 975, Golden Brown Dark).

How the list is made: a color is on this page when its DMC name contains one of these words: "yellow", "lemon", "canary", "topaz", "golden", "gold", "straw", "mustard", "cream", "tawny". Names are what DMC calls the color, so a few colors show up in two families (for example a "Blue Green" is on the green page and the blue page). Lightness is measured as CIELAB L*, the same color math the FlossChart converter uses.

The biggest yellow and gold color groups

Many DMC colors come in runs of one name from very light to very dark. In this family the biggest runs are: Topaz (8), Golden Olive (6), Golden Brown (5), Old Gold (5), Autumn Gold (4). The Topaz run, light to dark, is: Topaz Very Light (727), Topaz Light (726), Topaz Medium Light (725), Topaz (728), Topaz Medium (783), Topaz Dark (782), Topaz Very Dark (781), Topaz Ultra Very Dark (780).

The closest look-alikes in this family, by the same color difference: DMC 729 and DMC 3852 (difference 3.6); DMC 3821 and DMC 743 (difference 3.9); DMC 371 and DMC 370 (difference 4.2). On screen these are hard to tell apart, so check the real thread before you swap one for another.
